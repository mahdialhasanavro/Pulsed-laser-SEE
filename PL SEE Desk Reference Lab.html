Four hands-on explorers built from McMorrow et al., Pulsed-Laser Single-Event Effects (PL SEE) Testing: A Practical Desk Reference, DTRA-TR-23-43 (2023). Section numbers refer to that report. Models are simplified for learning. Hover any graph for readouts; tap graphs marked "tap" to set a value.
